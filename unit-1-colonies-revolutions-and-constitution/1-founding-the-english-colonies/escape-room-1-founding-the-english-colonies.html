Escape the Meetinghouse Loft

Founding the English Colonies

How to escape:
  1. Open each lock in order. Read the Clue Card. The answer is in the card.
  2. Solve the puzzle to open the lock and reveal one digit.
  3. If you get stuck, press Hint. Hints are free, but your teacher can see how many you used.
  4. Open all 5 locks, then type the 5-digit exit code. Finish with the Debrief.

You are locked in the meetinghouse loft.

The caretaker locked the meetinghouse loft for the night. A round window, an hourglass, a bell, a rack of scrolls, and a shelf of ledgers are in the room. The door has 5 locks. Each lock matches one big idea from Lesson 1. Solve them all to find the 5-digit exit code.

The clock starts when you press the button. Most students finish in about 20 minutes.
