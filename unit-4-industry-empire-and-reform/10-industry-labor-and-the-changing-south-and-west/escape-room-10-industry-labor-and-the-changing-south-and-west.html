How to escape:
  1. Open each lock in order. Read the Clue Card. The answer is in the card.
  2. Solve the puzzle to open the lock and reveal one digit.
  3. If you get stuck, press Hint. Hints are free, but your teacher can see how many you used.

You are locked in the switch tower.

The rail yard went quiet for the night and the switchman locked up the tower. Old train orders from 1859 to 1901 are still pinned to the wall. The door has 5 locks. Each lock matches one big idea from Lesson 10. Solve them all to find the 5-digit exit code.

The clock starts when you press the button. Most students finish in about 20 minutes.
