How to work (about 15 to 20 minutes):
  1. Each domino is an event with a date. Tap ▲ Up or ▼ Down to move it. (On a computer you can also drag it.)
  2. Tap Check my order. It tells you how many dominos are in the right place, but not which ones. You get 3 checks for each chain.
  3. Once your order is locked, write one “because” sentence for each link between two dominos.
  4. Answer the last question about which domino mattered most.
  5. Your work saves on this device as you go. Use Save Progress to download a backup and Load Progress to keep working later. When you are done, tap Finished and upload the file to Canvas.
