How to play: Each card has 3 statements about Lesson 7. Two are true. One is a lie. For every card:

  1. Pick the lie.
  2. Fix it. Write a true sentence in your own words.
  3. Prove it. Pick the Norton section that has the evidence.

Then press Save this card. If you caught the lie, you get a BUSTED stamp. If not, look again and try once more. Your work saves on this device as you go. Use Save Progress at the bottom to download a backup file, and Load Progress to continue later. Plan on about 15 to 20 minutes.
