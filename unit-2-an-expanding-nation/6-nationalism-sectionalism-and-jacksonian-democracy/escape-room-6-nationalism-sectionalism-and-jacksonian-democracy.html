Escape the Canal Toll House

Nationalism, Sectionalism, and Jacksonian Democracy

How to escape:
  1. Open each lock in order. Read the Clue Card. The answer is in the card.
  2. Solve the puzzle to open the lock and reveal one digit.
  3. If you get stuck, press Hint. Hints are free, but your teacher can see how many you used.
  4. Open all 5 locks, then type the 5-digit exit code. Finish with the Debrief.

You are locked in the canal toll house.

The canal toll house closed for the night, and the toll keeper locked the door. A shelf of ledgers, a brass scale, a lantern, a coil of rope, a cash box, and a wall clock sit in the room. The door has 5 locks. Each lock matches two big ideas from Lesson 6. Solve them all to find the 5-digit exit code.

The clock starts when you press the button. Most students finish in about 20 minutes.
