New Frontiers and a Great Society

How to escape:
  1. Open each lock in order. Read the Clue Card. The answer is in the card.
  2. Solve the puzzle to open the lock and reveal one digit.
  3. If you get stuck, press Hint. Hints are free, but your teacher can see how many you used.
  4. Open all 5 locks, then type the 5-digit exit code. Finish with the Debrief.

You are locked in the records office.

The town records office closed for the night, and the clerk locked the door. A filing cabinet, a typewriter, a rubber stamp, a desk telephone, and a ballot box from the 1960s sit in the room. The door has 5 locks. Each lock matches one big idea from Lesson 18. Solve them all to find the 5-digit exit code.

The clock starts when you press the button. Most students finish in about 20 minutes.
