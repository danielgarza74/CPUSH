Escape the Print Shop

Rebellion and Reaction

How to escape:
  1. Open each lock in order. Read the Clue Card. The answer is in the card.
  2. Solve the puzzle to open the lock and reveal one digit.
  3. If you get stuck, press Hint. Hints are free, but your teacher can see how many you used.
  4. Open all 5 locks, then type the 5-digit exit code. Finish with the Debrief.

You are locked in the print shop.

The community center print shop closed for the night, and the volunteer locked the door. A mimeograph (an old copy machine), a clothesline of flyers, a shelf of ink jars, a rack of paper, and a supply table from the 1970s sit in the room. The door has 5 locks. Each lock matches one or two big ideas from Lesson 19. Solve them all to find the 5-digit exit code.

The clock starts when you press the button. Most students finish in about 20 minutes.
